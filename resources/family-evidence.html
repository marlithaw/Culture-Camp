Why Partner With Families?

Trust Is The
Feedback Loop

Families are critical partners in culture. Calls strengthen trust and create a two-way loop between school and home. Tied to agency practice and psychological safety: when families feel like partners, students feel safer to grow.

Call Type Definitions

Three Private,
Scripted Calls

Shout-Out

Celebrate specific evidence of the student living a value.

Growth

Identify one concrete skill to practice — together, at home and school.

Partnership

Collaborate on support and a clear follow-up plan.
