Restorative Repair Overview

Accountability
And Belonging

Punitive discipline removes belonging to create accountability. Restorative repair holds both: the student owns the harm and stays in the community. That is what turns an incident into a lesson.

Perry's Student-Facing Sequence

Regulate · Relate · Reason

Regulate
Relate
Reason
Regulate

Calm the nervous system first. No reasoning with a dysregulated brain. Adult capacity required: R³ Reflection.

Relate

Empathize and listen to understand. People repair when they feel understood. Adult capacity required: R³ Resistance.

Reason

Collaboratively decide how to make it right. Adult capacity required: R³ Revelation.

Perry's 3 R's name what the student needs. R³ Agency Practice (Reflection · Resistance · Revelation) is the adult-side capacity that makes each step deliverable. R³ is the precondition for Perry's sequence, not a renaming of it.

Repair Conversation Scripts

Name The Harm,
Not The Character

Open With Care

"I want to understand what happened, and I want us to make it right together."

Name The Harm

"When ___ happened, it affected ___. I'm not asking you to feel bad — I'm asking us to repair it."

Seek Agreement

"What would help make this right? Here's what I can do, and here's what I'm asking of you."

Avoid This

"Say sorry." — Forced apologies teach performance, not repair.
