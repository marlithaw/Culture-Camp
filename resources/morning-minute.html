The Routine

The Four Sparks

1
Greeting

Every student greeted by name. Eye contact, a handshake, a hello in their language.

2
Sharing

One voice at a time. Students share, peers respond — building empathy and listening.

3
Activity

A quick, joyful group game that builds team and energy for the day ahead.

4
Message

A shared morning message that previews the day and names what we're working toward.
