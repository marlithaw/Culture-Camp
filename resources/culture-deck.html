Purpose

Teach The Routine

Culture Camp prepares students and teachers for daily practice. Without it, the routine never takes root. These ten days are where students learn the sequence cold — so it holds under pressure later.

Artifact · Student Practice Plan08

Days 7–9 · Transfer Ownership

Reteach & Student Leadership

Reteach What's Shaky

Diagnose the weak move and reteach it explicitly. Do not move on from a broken step.

Hand It To Students

Rotate roles and let students run more of the routine each day. Ownership is the goal.
