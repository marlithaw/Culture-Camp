What Is Friday Cypher?

A Circle Where
Every Voice Is Lifted

Rooted in hip-hop, the cypher is a circle of mutual respect where the community amplifies each voice. Here, the week's documented evidence becomes public recognition — celebration that protects dignity instead of ranking students.

The Principle

Recognition Is
Evidence, Not
Popularity.

Nomination & Evidence

Recognition Earns
Its Way On Stage

  • Nominations come from documented Morning Meeting participation and practice.
  • Evidence is a specific behavior aligned to Safe, Respectful, or Responsible.
  • "Was nice" is not evidence. Name the value, the behavior, and the impact.

Not A
Popularity
Contest.

Strong vs Weak Nominations

Make The
Standard Clear

ValueStrong — Specific EvidenceWeak
Responsible"Returned the shared materials and reset the space without being asked.""Was helpful."
Respectful"Used an 'add-on' frame to build on a peer's idea instead of talking over.""Is nice."
Safe"Asked for a break using the routine, then returned ready to learn.""Behaved well."
