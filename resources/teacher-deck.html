Teacher Training04

What Culture Lab Is

Structured Practice,
Not Circle Time

It Is
  • A 15-minute daily rehearsal of Safe, Respectful, Responsible.
  • A fixed six-move sequence that never changes.
  • Practice of one observable culture move each day.
It Is Not
  • Open-ended chat or free venting.
  • A reward or a behavior chart.
  • Optional on busy days.

Teacher Training07

Moves 1–2 · Regulate & Orient

Settle The Body, Name The Day

Do
Cue students to the meeting space; settle bodies; reveal the Message Board (day, value, action, first task).
Say
"Feet flat, hands still, eyes here. Today our value is ____. Our action step is ____."
Look For
Bodies settling within 30 seconds; eyes on the board; readiness to begin.
Record
Note who needed a second cue; tap whole-group readiness.
Reset If Needed
Re-cue calmly with fewer words; offer a private regulation option before continuing.
Teacher Training08

Moves 3–4 · Connect & Practice

Structured Voice, Then Rehearse

Do
Pose the share prompt; partners share with frames; two voices share out. Then teach and rehearse one culture move.
Say
"A strong share is clear, connected, and concise. Now we practice — this is something we do, not just say."
Look For
Both partners speaking; frames used; the practice move performed, not just described.
Record
Tap participation; note standout practice-move evidence for recognition.
Reset If Needed
Re-model the frame; shorten the share; pair a quiet student with a steady partner.
Teacher Training09

Moves 5–6 · Commit & Transition

Lock It In, Then Stand On The One

Do
Students name how they will apply the value before lunch. Close by naming value, action, and first academic task.
Say
"Before lunch, I will ____. Value: ____. Action: ____. First task: ____. Stand on the one."
Look For
Specific, personal commitments; a clean, quiet transition into academics.
Record
Capture a sample of commitments; note transition time.
Reset If Needed
Offer a sentence frame for the commitment; re-cue the transition without raising your voice.

What To Track

Seconds,
Not Paperwork

CaptureHow
ParticipationOne tap for whole-group share completion.
Practice MoveTap students who performed the move; note standouts.
Recognition EvidenceJot a specific behavior tied to a value — feeds Friday Cypher.
Reset / RepairNote if a reset or repair happened, for follow-up only.
Teacher Training14

Troubleshooting

Tighten The Structure,
Not Your Voice

SnagQuick Fix
Low energyShorten the share; add a movement-based practice move.
Dominant talkerUse the frame "let's add a new voice"; assign a mic passer.
SilenceGive think time and a sentence frame before calling on anyone.
Running longUse a student time keeper; trim to one share-out.
Teacher Training15

When The Routine Breaks Down

Lean On It,
Don't Abandon It

  • Regulate first — your calm is the intervention.
  • Fewer words as escalation rises.
  • Cue the reset privately: a quiet whisper or non-verbal signal.
  • Repair only after regulation.

We Correct
The Routine,
Not The Child.
